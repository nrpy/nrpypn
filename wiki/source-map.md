## Scope

This map records exact registered paths and direct source-to-page links. It is
not a claim that every expression coefficient or external derivation has been
independently verified. `exact seed` describes the cited portion of a living
file; `registered` identifies material without a filed compiled-page dependency.
Follow the covered scope and known gaps before expanding an answer.

Frozen NRPy convention documents guide structure and maintenance. NRPyPN source
code, tests, and project documentation decide repository facts according to the
claim's role. Current local governance supersedes older tracking instructions
in preserved evidence. The frozen text stays verbatim.

On source drift, follow the direct dependent pages and their typed neighbors,
check glossary terms and catalog aliases, and reconcile the owning claims.
Update this map when a durable source dependency changes. Page agreement is not
independent scientific evidence; no source fingerprints are required.
